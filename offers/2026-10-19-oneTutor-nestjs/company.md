# OneTutor: research for the cover letter

_Found by claude-opus-5-5 on 2026-10-19; check before sending._

- OneTutor answers learner questions only from the institution's approved course materials. Each answer cites its source (slide or page), and when the material doesn't cover a question the tutor says so instead of guessing. ([source](https://onetutor.ai))
- Educators upload PDFs, text, video and audio. They can switch individual documents on or off and must approve exercises before they are published. Anonymised analytics show where a cohort struggles. ([source](https://onetutor.ai))
- The site describes OneTutor as LLM-agnostic, built on retrieval over curated content (RAG), GDPR compliant, and integrated through SSO and LTI 1.3 (e.g. Moodle, Ilias). ([source](https://onetutor.ai))
- The site claims 50+ institutions, 45,000+ learners and 2.8 million learning interactions. Named customers include TUM, Leibniz Universität Hannover, Charles University, WU Wien, Nanyang Technological University and Deutsches Museum. ([source](https://onetutor.ai))
- OneTutor started in a TUM project course and was founded in May 2025 by five students. It was supported by the TUM Startup Advisory Office and TUM Venture Lab Software & AI, and Professor Alexander Pretschner is named as a co-founder. ([source](https://www.tum.de/en/news-and-events/all-news/press-releases/details/successful-study-with-ai))
- TUM's press release reports 245,000+ questions asked to the AI tutor and 400,000+ quiz questions answered. In the pilot, active users had a noticeably higher exam pass rate. A three-year bidt study will evaluate it in about 100 lectures at 10 Bavarian universities. ([source](https://www.tum.de/en/news-and-events/all-news/press-releases/details/successful-study-with-ai))
- Built In lists OneTutor at about 5 employees. ([source](https://builtin.com/company/onetutor))
