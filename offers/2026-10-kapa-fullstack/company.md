# kapa.ai: research for the cover letter

_Found by claude-opus-5-5 on 2026-10-19; check before sending._

- kapa.ai turns a company's knowledge (docs, tickets, wikis and other sources) into AI agents that answer questions for users and staff, and offers a retrieval API and a hosted MCP server so teams can use its search in their own agents. ([source](https://www.kapa.ai/))
- Used by 200+ companies; named customers include OpenAI, Logitech, monday.com, Nokia, Raspberry Pi and Nordic Semiconductor (e.g. Nordic Semiconductor: 700k+ queries answered, 83% lower query token cost). ([source](https://www.kapa.ai/))
- Deployment options include a website 'Ask AI' widget, Slack and Discord bots, a Zendesk agent/app, a support form deflector, and analytics on coverage gaps; knowledge comes from 50+ source types. ([source](https://www.kapa.ai/))
- kapa.ai's blog post 'RAG Gone Wrong' lists common mistakes, including relying on vector similarity alone without a reranker, and having no evals; it recommends testing retrieval before generation, since weak retrieval can't be fully compensated by a stronger LLM. ([source](https://www.kapa.ai/blog/rag-gone-wrong-the-7-most-common-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them))
- Y Combinator Summer 2023 company, founded 2023 by Emil Soerensen (CEO) and Finn Bauer (CTO), team of 22; several roles are remote across many European countries. ([source](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kapa-ai))
- Raised a $3.2M seed round in October 2024 led by Initialized Capital, with Y Combinator and angels including Douwe Kiela, Amjad Masad and Solomon Hykes. ([source](https://capacitymedia.com/smarter-technical-ai-support-assistants))
