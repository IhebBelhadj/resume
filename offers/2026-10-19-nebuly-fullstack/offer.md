About NebulyNebuly is a fast-growing, VC-backed startup building the platform that proves the ROI of enterprise AI.
Every enterprise function — sales, customer support, HR, finance — is deploying AI agents at scale, and budgets are following fast. But most companies can't answer a basic question: is this spend actually working?Every enterprise deploying AI agents is asking the same question: is this actually delivering value? Today, almost none of them can answer it. The signal that could answer it is right there — millions of conversations happening every day between employees, customers, and these agents — but it's unstructured, high-volume, and invisible without the right infrastructure. So the ROI question stays unanswered, not because the answer doesn't exist, but because no one can see it. Nebuly makes that signal visible. It sits on top of any enterprise AI deployment, turns those conversations into a clear answer — is this working, and where — and connects the dots back to ROI. Think of it as the accountability layer for enterprise AI: connect once, see the impact, prove the value — with no data ever leaving your environment.
Every new data category has created a new software category. CRM turned customer interactions into pipeline accountability. Product analytics turned user behavior into growth accountability. Nebuly is building that same layer for AI — turning millions of invisible conversations into a clear answer on value.

What you’ll doAs a Full Stack Engineer, your primary focus will be designing, developing, and maintaining both front-end and back-end components, ensuring seamless integration between client-side interfaces and back-end services. You will work across the full stack, from database and API design to user-facing interfaces. Your responsibilities will include:
Developing robust front-end solutions using modern web technologies such as TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and popular frameworks like React and Vite.
Designing and building back-end services and APIs using languages such as Python, ensuring scalability, reliability, and maintainability.
Implementing intuitive and responsive user interfaces with a focus on delivering an optimal user experience across various devices.
Designing and managing data models and complex queries across relational and analytics-focused databases such as PostgreSQL and ClickHouse.
Optimizing performance across the stack to ensure rapid load times, efficient APIs, and responsiveness end-to-end.
Conduct thorough testing and debugging of both front-end and back-end code to ensure high-quality software that meets functional and design requirements.
Participate in code reviews, provide constructive feedback, and collaborate on technical discussions.
Work in plenty of autonomy and take full ownership of your work, from ideation to execution.
