# Phantasma Labs: research for the cover letter

_Found by claude-opus-5-5 on 2026-10-19; check before sending._

- Berlin-based Phantasma Labs closed a seven-figure investment from Momenta and RunwayFBU, announced 8 October 2024. It already had customers in the DACH region and said the money would fund expansion into the US and the Nordics. ([source](https://www.newswire.com/news/momenta-and-runwayfbu-invest-in-phantasma-labs-22437740))
- Its production planning software is built on reinforcement learning. The company says it produces plans up to 30% better and 100 times faster than traditional methods, and that it does not depend on large datasets, which opens it to SMEs, mid-caps and large enterprises. ([source](https://www.newswire.com/news/momenta-and-runwayfbu-invest-in-phantasma-labs-22437740))
- Ramakrishna Nanjundaiah is the founder and CEO. The investor describes the team as combining AI/ML, large-scale simulation and high-performance computing. ([source](https://www.newswire.com/news/momenta-and-runwayfbu-invest-in-phantasma-labs-22437740))
- The AI trains on simulated factory events such as staff absences, machine breakdowns and sudden demand spikes. The company says a use case takes about four weeks to develop and can be deployed on-premise or in the cloud. ([source](https://theinnovator.news/?p=10714))
- Customers include automotive tier-one and tier-two suppliers, large OEMs and machine tool manufacturers. The company launched in 2020 with a focus on automotive and moved to the wider industrial sector in 2023. ([source](https://theinnovator.news/?p=10714))
- The job posting says the product, AiPS, combines reinforcement learning with Digital Twin environments. Phantasma works with manufacturers across Europe, the US and Japan, partners with ERP and MES providers, and is a small international team in Berlin with flexible remote work. ([source](https://join.com/companies/phantasma/15848301-software-engineer))
