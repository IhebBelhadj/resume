# Oneleet: research for the cover letter

_Found by claude-opus-5-5 on 2026-10-19; check before sending._

- Oneleet is a security and compliance platform for B2B software companies seeking SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS and other certifications. It combines compliance automation with code security scanning, human-led penetration testing, attack surface discovery and vCISO services. ([source](https://www.oneleet.com/))
- Oneleet integrates with cloud, code and workplace tools such as AWS, GitHub, Google Workspace, Slack and Okta to gather evidence and find gaps. One control set maps across several frameworks. ([source](https://www.oneleet.com/))
- The homepage claims 2,000+ companies secured, 300+ teams migrated from Vanta, Drata or Secureframe, and a 4.9/5 rating on G2. ([source](https://www.oneleet.com/))
- Oneleet was founded in 2022 by former penetration tester Bryan Onel, Ora Onel and Erik Vogelzang. It was in Y Combinator's Summer 2022 batch. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/02/oneleet-raises-33m-to-shake-up-the-world-of-security-compliance))
- Onel criticises platforms that only collect evidence and issue certificates as 'compliance theater', because companies can be certified and still be vulnerable. Oneleet bundles real pentests and code scanning instead. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/02/oneleet-raises-33m-to-shake-up-the-world-of-security-compliance))
- Oneleet raised a $33M Series A led by Dawn Capital, bringing its total to $35M. Other backers include Y Combinator, Arash Ferdowsi and Frank Slootman. The money will go to expanding engineering, AI capabilities and customer reach. TechCrunch reports $9M ARR. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/02/oneleet-raises-33m-to-shake-up-the-world-of-security-compliance))
- Oneleet is based in Amsterdam. Its Series A closed in October 2025. ([source](https://tech.eu/2025/10/02/oneleet-lands-33m-series-a-from-dawn-capital//))
